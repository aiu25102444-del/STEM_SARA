About STEM SARA

Empowering students through technology, innovation, and meaningful social impact.

Who We Are

STEM SARA is a student-led initiative founded by Afghan Computer Science students at Albukhary International University who are passionate about technology, education, and social impact.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower young minds through STEM education, technology, and innovation while contributing toward a future with zero unemployment. Through learning, collaboration, and creativity, STEM SARA aims to inspire students, especially girls, to grow into skilled and socially responsible future professionals.

Our Vision

A future where students are empowered with technology, confidence, leadership, and opportunities to create positive change in society.

What STEM SARA Stands For

SARA is a persian word representing a center or hub while STEM stands for Science, technology, engineering and mathematics, together STEM_SARA means center for STEM fields.

Our Values & Goals

Innovation

Encouraging creativity and problem-solving through technology and STEM education.

Collaboration

Building teamwork and community engagement through shared learning experiences.

Empowerment

Supporting students, especially girls, to grow into confident future leaders.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can join STEM SARA?

Any student passionate about technology, innovation, education, and social impact is welcome to join.

What activities does STEM SARA organize?

STEM_SARA organizes workshops, projects, awareness campaigns, educational initiatives, and collaborative student activities.
